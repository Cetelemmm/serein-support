Your session

Set minutes and seconds separately, in five-second steps. Settling-in time is separate from meditation time. Your last duration and language are saved.

Lock screen

On a supported iPhone, allow Live Activities for the timer and, on iOS 26, the end alarm. Earlier versions use notifications, whose sounds depend on silent mode and Focus settings. A fully dark screen cannot display the timer without Always-On hardware.

Free and lifetime access

Up to 15 minutes without ads. For a longer session, an optional ad can unlock the session before settling-in time. No ads during meditation. If an ad is unavailable, the session may start for free. Serein for life is a one-time purchase, with no subscription: all durations without ads, two extra scenes, two characters and two gongs. The planned French price is EUR9.99; Apple displays the local price. This offer is being configured; the public launch has not happened yet.

Restore and rewards

Restore purchases in Settings → Serein for life using the same Apple account. Rewards for 7 and 30 consecutive days and 100 hours remain free. Your history and rewards stay on your device.

Your data

The policy below explains collection and deletion requests. Settings → Privacy and my data allows permanent deletion of the pseudonymous account and server data after confirmation, when this service is active. Local history and Apple purchases are kept.

Privacy

Controller and contact

Hugo Barbé — hugo.barbe.pro@gmail.com. Contact this address for access, correction, objection or deletion requests. Your data identifier is shown in the app.

Local data

Duration, language, settings and history are saved on your device. Apple handles purchases. Serein does not receive your payment card details.

Usage and feedback

Supabase receives app opens, session stages and durations, platform and timestamps with a pseudonymous identifier. Earlier local history is not uploaded. Feedback is sent only on your explicit action; an optional email can accompany a future offer request. Pseudonymous usage data helps improve Serein; it is not anonymous.

Advertising

An ad request occurs only after you choose to watch an ad before a long session. Google AdMob may process identifiers, technical data, advertising interactions, diagnostics and approximate location derived from IP addresses. Requested ads are non-personalized. Google’s consent SDK presents applicable choices before advertising initialization. No cross-app tracking is requested. Choices are available in Settings → Serein for life.

Retention and deletion

Usage events older than 90 days and feedback/emails older than 12 months are deleted daily from the server. Pseudonymous accounts remain until deletion is requested. Account deletion also deletes its feedback and usage data and stops automatic collection on this device. Uninstalling does not itself remove server data. Apple transaction data follows Apple’s policies.

Recipients

The Serein team can view feedback and usage in Supabase. Server rules prohibit access to another user’s data. Supabase, Google for advertising and Apple for purchases also apply their own policies. Serein does not use advertising data to build medical profiles.

Your rights

Contact the controller above. You may also complain to the French data protection authority CNIL if your rights are not respected. This meditation app does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.
